Questions

Before you enquire

I am relocating and cannot visit yet. Can I still start?

Yes. Use the enquiry form, add where you are moving from, and choose Video tour in the tour step. You can plan areas, commute, and home styles before you ever fly in.

I am an investor. What should I put in the form?

Your budget in USD, the areas you are looking at, your timeline, and in the notes whether the goal is a rental, a long term hold, or both. That gives the first conversation a clear starting point.

Does sending an enquiry commit me to anything?

No. It is a first conversation starter. You choose how you want to be contacted, and you can stop at any point.

I want to sell my Cedar Park home. Is valuation still available?

Yes. The current profile offers a valuation request by phone and email. In the full build, that becomes a short form on this page as well, so sellers get the same one-step experience as buyers.
